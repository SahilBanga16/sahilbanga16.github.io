Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user personal assistant used only by its owner, Sahil Banga. It is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, it reads and manages his own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts. It uses this access to summarise email, track his schedule and organise his files.
